Cancellation & Refunds

Last Updated: June 24, 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled by the canteen administration (for example, in cases where items are sold out or the kitchen is closed). Students cannot cancel an order once the payment is completed and order has entered processing.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are initiated only under the following conditions:

  • An order is cancelled by the admin due to item stock out.
  • Payment was deducted from your account, but the order failed to create or finalize.
  • Technical glitches resulting in duplicate charges.

3. Refund Process

If eligible, refunds will be initiated automatically to your original payment source (via Razorpay). Please allow 5-7 working days for the amount to reflect in your bank account/UPI account.

4. Support

For any refund queries or support requests, please contact us at kitcbecanteen@gmail.com or call our helpline with your Order ID and payment transaction reference.
