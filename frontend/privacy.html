Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect details you provide when registering and using Canteen Express, including your name, email address, password hash, and details of orders you place.

2. How We Use Information

Your details are used solely to authenticate your identity, process your orders, contact you about updates, and ensure transaction security.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties. We share transaction details securely with Razorpay to process payments. We employ standard industry safeguards to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Cookies

We utilize local browser storage (such as localStorage) to keep you logged in between sessions. No advertising tracking cookies are used.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your data or our privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
